# Sprint 1 Effort Estimation System

Effort is estimated in total person-hours.

| Effort | Estimated total work |
|---|---:|
| Low | 1–3 hours |
| Medium | 4–8 hours |
| High | 9+ hours |

## Rules
- Estimate the work required to complete the Issue, including implementation,
  testing, review, and documentation.
- Use total person-hours when more than one person works on an Issue.
- Every planned Sprint 1 Issue receives one effort label.
- The effort label and the Effort field in the Sprint 1 Plan must match.
