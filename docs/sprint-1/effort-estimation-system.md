# Sprint 1 Effort Estimation System

## Purpose
This system helps the CareerConnect team estimate how much work each Sprint 1 item requires.

## Estimation unit
Effort is estimated in total person-hours.

If two team members each work for three hours, the total effort is six hours.

## Effort scale

| Effort level | Estimated work |
|---|---:|
| Low | 1-3 hours |
| Medium | 4-8 hours |
| High | 9 or more hours |

## Estimation rules
- Estimate implementation, testing, review, and documentation work.
- Use total person-hours when more than one member works on an item.
- Estimate only work planned for Sprint 1.
- Review estimates during team meetings if the scope changes.
- Record the chosen effort level in the Sprint 1 Plan view.

## Examples

| Work item | Estimated effort |
|---|---|
| Team Capacity document | Low |
| Registration form and validation | Medium |
| Complete login feature with testing | High |
